

#CThe Creatures
#Cof Night

#CBy Hubertus
#Cthe Bald

#Ctranslated from Latin
#Cby his brother in prayer
#CFratre Johan Markus

#P#T#TOf Monstrosity

#TYou who read me, know that night
engenders monsters and that night
creatures exist. The accursed book
of Abdul Al Azred is clear on this
matter:
"That is not dead which can eternal lie."
Unhappy he who knows that book.
#PUnhappy he whose eyes alight upon
that foulest of texts.
Unhappy he who implores the
standing stones.
For he will free the powers of darkness.
#P#T#TOf the Pit

#TStagnant waters are like the memory of
men. Beneath the surface calm, clawed
beasts await and are known to initiates
as the Deep Ones.
#PAwaiting his prey, the Deep One seizes
him and drags him down to the abyss
where Dagon the cruel god swims and
reveres him whose name may not be
pronounced.
#P#T#TOf Libraries

#TUnhappy he who frees the prowler.
Unhappy he who meets the prowler erring
among the books. He generates the
vagabond that comes from other spheres.
He believes the vagabond does not exist.
#PHe will feel the embrace of death for, in
the eyes of the vagabond, books are no
more than dreams, stone no more than wind.
The vagabond knows how to take the
breath of the reckless.
#P#T#TOf Strife

#THe who speaks does not know and believes
he is able to kill the creatures of the
night. Folly. Evil is conjured up by
science and secrecy.
PHe who prowls among
books will perish by the blade. He who
flies in the dark caverns will scream in
fear. He who swims in the depths will
evaporate. But he who believes he knows,
knows nothing. He who knows, says nothing.
#P#T#TOf Death

#TThere are domains more terrible than
death. That is not dead which can eternal
lie. Each creature is conjured up and
is not dead but returns to the origins.
A monster, a science. Steel kills the
vagabond who never dies.
#P
