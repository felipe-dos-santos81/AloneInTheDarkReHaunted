
#CUnfinished Chapter of
#CTerra Incognita
#Cby Jacob Van Ostadte.


#CA hitherto unpublished fragment
#Cof the manuscript, unearthed
#Cfollowing indications furnished
#Cin the Vatican library's
#CExpurgatory Index
#PIn those icy and unwelcoming lands, the
rites of wizards and healers are deeply
rooted in ancient legend. Mysterious and
cruel beings are thought to have ruled
over the Arctic plains in times past. A
cursed city, enclosed by massive walls,
is believed to stand to this day. It
contains fabulous treasures and is
inhabited by the degenerate descendants
of those who instilled centuries of
terror in the hearts of the people.
#PThese people, naturally placid, are
seized with rage and horror at the very
mention of the "Prisoners of the Ice".
Were these dreadful captives to be freed
from their frozen cells, they would reap
a horrifying tribute of human flesh.
These blood-curdling beings may be
invoked by certain ritual words. They
can even be controlled, albeit with the
greatest of difficulty.
#PI admit to being impressed by these
tales, repeated to me on many occasions
and in a number of different places
during my travels in the region. I have
also seen troubling cult objects,
sculpted in a material unknown to me.
Another remarkable fact is that local
Eskimos experience great distaste in
pronouncing certain words and invariably
avoid saying them.
#PHere is a living
example of the power that words contain.
As it is said in the Bible: In the
beginning was the word.
