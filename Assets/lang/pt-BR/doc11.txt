#PNumerous devilish cults speak of
monstrous creatures called the Old
Ones. These supernatural beings are
believed to be possessed of powers
equivalent to those of the gods of
antique religions. Adepts of such cults
refer to forbidden literature in order
to cause these frightful entities to
appear before them.
#PWhat serious student
of folk myths has not come across the
names of Cthulhu and Shub Niggurath?
#PIt must be said that these creatures
wield tremendous power and are difficult
to control once they have been unleashed
into the world.
Those who serve "He who goes in shadows"
protect themselves with signs of stone,
carved into the walls of houses or
engraved on various objects.
#PFor these misguided servants of evil,
the best protection appears to be that
afforded by "the sign of the most
ancient gods", engraved in MNAR stone, a
heavy material, said to be disagreeable
to the touch.

#PThe sinful practices of those who fall
into such errors can only lead to the
darkest of despair and are a mortal
danger to the soul.
Such monsters as those invoked by these
foolhardy individuals are engendered
when reason drops its guard.
Man is easily tempted into perversion.
#PIt is why we must forever remain alert
and renounce Satan with each breath we
take. His ways are infinite in number.
